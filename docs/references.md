# References and further reading

This page lists the resources that informed the guidance in this documentation, and the online tools used in worked examples.

## Tools used in the examples

- [invoicegenerator.run](https://invoicegenerator.run/) - the online invoice generator used to test the templates and field layouts described in {doc}`quickstart` and {doc}`field-reference`. It is a good fit for freelancers and small businesses that need clean PDFs without learning full accounting software.
- [Sphinx](https://www.sphinx-doc.org/) - the documentation generator that builds this site.
- [MyST Parser](https://myst-parser.readthedocs.io/) - the Markdown flavour used in these pages.
- [Read the Docs](https://about.readthedocs.com/) - the platform that hosts this documentation.
- [JSON Schema](https://json-schema.org/) - the specification used for the invoice data model in {doc}`schema`.

## Standards and specifications

- The European Commission's overview of [invoicing rules](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/taxation/value-added-tax-vat/vat-invoicing-rules_en) gives the canonical list of required fields for VAT invoices across the EU.
- The UK government's guidance on [invoicing and taking payment](https://www.gov.uk/invoicing-and-taking-payment-from-customers) summarises the requirements for invoices issued by UK businesses.
- The US Internal Revenue Service [Self-Employed Individuals Tax Center](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/self-employed-individuals-tax-center) is the starting point for US-based freelancers.
- [PEPPOL](https://peppol.org/) is the cross-border electronic invoicing network referenced in {doc}`faq`.
- The [Universal Business Language (UBL)](http://docs.oasis-open.org/ubl/) is the OASIS standard underpinning much of electronic invoicing; the schema in {doc}`schema` is a small subset of the same idea.

## Books and articles

- *Profit First* by Mike Michalowicz. A simple cash-allocation system that pairs well with consistent invoicing habits.
- *The Freelancer's Bible* by Sara Horowitz. Practical advice on freelance business operations including invoicing and collections.
- *Small Time Operator* by Bernard Kamoroff. A long-running primer on US small-business tax and bookkeeping.

These are general-audience references and we receive nothing from listing them.

## Disclaimer

Nothing on this site is legal, tax or financial advice. Always check with a qualified professional in your jurisdiction before relying on a specific field, rate or procedure described here. Invoice rules change, exchange rates fluctuate, and the regulatory environment for small businesses is not uniform across countries or even across states within a country.

## How to contribute references

If you know of a primary source, a standard or a well-written guide that fits the spirit of this documentation, open a pull request on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/wsmhj/open-invoice-docs) adding it to the appropriate section above with a short, neutral description.
